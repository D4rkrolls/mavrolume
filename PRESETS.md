# Film recipes

Mavrolume includes 34 independently authored looks. The first 19 emphasize stronger color and monochrome palettes; the remaining 15 use a smoother tonal path. Each recipe has a suggested texture and tone setting, and every adjustment remains editable in the camera.

Quality controls grain amount and size, softness, bloom, halation and subtle color fringing. Tone controls contrast, saturation, dynamic range and midtones. The same GPU shader processes the live viewfinder and saved JPEGs.
